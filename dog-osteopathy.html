Meet Julie Garreau

Trained in France.

Julie holds a degree in animal osteopathy from France, validated by the National Council of the Order of Veterinarians in France. She may use animal osteopathy, dry needling and cold laser therapy within her training and professional scope.

Owners may consider it for dogs showing stiffness, reduced flexibility, slower rising, sensitivity after activity or ageing-related mobility changes.

Convenient for regulars

Booked alongside daycare or a hotel stay.

Contact Woofland to check upcoming consultation availability.

Important note. Julie Garreau is not registered as an osteopath in New Zealand. Animal osteopathy consultations at Woofland do not replace veterinary diagnosis, veterinary treatment or emergency veterinary care. If your dog is unwell, injured or in pain, please contact your veterinarian first.
