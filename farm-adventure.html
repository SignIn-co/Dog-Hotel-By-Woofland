Farm Farm
Woofland / Farm Adventure
An add-on to daycare

A real farm.Not just a walk.

For suitable daycare dogs: at least two hours with paws on the farm, with supervised outdoor play, movement, enrichment and exploration.

How it works

Morning routine, then open fields.

1

Start at daycare

Book a normal daycare day, then add Farm Adventure before submitting your booking. Dogs follow their usual morning routine at our indoor branch.

2

Two hours on the ground

Eligible dogs travel in the Woof Van and spend at least two hours at the farm. Travel time is not included.

3

Washed and rested

Dogs are washed as needed and return to the indoor branch for rest, comfort and mental stimulation before going home.

Who is it for? Daycare dogs who enjoy outdoor environments, sniffing, exploring, running and supervised social play. Suitability depends on temperament, age, confidence, health and behaviour.
