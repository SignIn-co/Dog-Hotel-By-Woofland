How Woofland began

Why does good dog care have to feel so restrictive?

That question started Woofland. A couple planning a trip, their dog Hapu, and a search for somewhere that felt like home without giving up the standards of a professional facility.

So we built it. Purpose-built spaces in place of cages. Calm routines in place of isolation. Dogs treated as individuals, by people who are genuinely there.
