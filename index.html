Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Are all dog breeds welcome at Woofland?

Absolutely. We welcome all dog breeds, as long as they are friendly, social and up to date with their vaccinations. Each dog is assessed before joining group play.

What are your operating hours?

Our standard hours are 7:00 am to 7:00 pm, Monday to Friday. Boarding drop-offs and pick-ups are available by arrangement.

Do you provide overnight care?

Yes. We offer both home-like stays and private pods for overnight boarding, depending on your dog's needs.

Can my dog join the Farm Adventure from Wairau?

Yes. Dogs attending daycare at Wairau can enjoy a free transfer to our farm for the Farm Adventure experience when booked in advance.

Which vaccinations does my dog need?

Your dog must be up to date with core vaccinations, including kennel cough, to attend Woofland.

What should I bring for my dog's stay?

Please bring their bed, food, favourite toy and any necessary medication, so they feel comfortable and at home.
