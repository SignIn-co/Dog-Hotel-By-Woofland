What makes it different

While you sleep, someone sleeps beside them.

Overnight, a team member stays and sleeps in the Pack Lounge, providing constant supervision, reassurance and a familiar human presence. Staff are present in the building at all times, day and night.

No cages. No crates. No empty building.
Stay your way

Three ways to stay.

Different dogs rest differently. We match the room and routine to temperament, social comfort and wellbeing.

01

Pack LoungeRelax. Socialise. Feel at home.

A shared, home-style space with couches and soft bedding for dogs who enjoy company. Overnight, a team member sleeps in the lounge with them.

$90per night. $80 for regular daycare dogs.
02

Paw PodCalm. Secure. Private.

A cosy private pod for dogs who rest best alone. Climate controlled with monitored airflow, comfortable bedding and gentle lighting.

$70per night. $63 for regular daycare dogs.
03

Alpha SuiteSpacious. Quiet. Comfortable.

Our most spacious and private option, with natural daylight and room for dogs from the same family to stay together. Well suited to longer stays.
